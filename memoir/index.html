How to use this

  1. Tap the big green circle.
  2. If your phone or computer asks to use the microphone, tap Allow.
  3. Talk normally. Your words appear on the screen.
  4. Tap the circle again when you are done.
  5. Check the box it picked for you — change it if it guessed wrong — and tap Save It.

Settings

Save to Google Drive

Off. Your writing stays on this device only.

Use my own Google set-up

Paste a Google OAuth client id to point this app at your own Google project. The README explains how to make one — it takes about five minutes and is free.

Keep your writing safe

Your writing lives in this browser's storage. If the browsing data is ever cleared it goes with it, so make a backup now and then — email it to someone you trust, or keep it on a memory stick.
