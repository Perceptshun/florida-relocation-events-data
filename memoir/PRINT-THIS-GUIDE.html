My Life Stories

Say it out loud. It writes it down for you.

To write something

  1. Tap the app icon — the green circle with a microphone.
  2. Tap the 🎤 Press to Start Talking circle in the middle.
  3. Talk normally. Your words appear on the screen as you go. You can pause and think — it keeps listening.
  4. Tap the circle again when you are finished. It turns green again.
  5. Look at the three boxes underneath. The app has picked one for you. If it picked wrong, tap the right one.
  6. Tap ✓ Save It. That's it — it's in your book.

The three books

👤 About My LifeAnything about you — where you grew up, the work you did, the people you loved.
👥 About Someone RealA true story about somebody else — your father, a friend, a neighbour.
📖 A Made-Up StorySomething you invented — a character, a tale.

Saying punctuation

Say “period”puts a . in
Say “comma”puts a , in
Say “question mark”puts a ? in
Say “new paragraph”starts a new paragraph
You don't have to. If you just talk, it puts the full stops in for you.

The four buttons along the bottom

🎤 TalkWhere you do your talking.
📚 WritingEverything you have written. You can read it, fix a word, hear it read aloud, or print it.
✓ StreakA calendar. Every day you write something, that day gets a ✓.
❓ HelpBigger text, night colours, and backups.

If something looks wrong

Words are too smallTap A+ at the top right. Tap it again for bigger still.
Nothing appears when you talkTap the circle so it is green again, then tap it once more and start talking. If the screen asks about the microphone, tap Allow.
It filed something in the wrong bookGo to 📚 Writing, find it, tap ↔️ Move, and pick the right book.
A word came out wrongGo to 📚 Writing, tap ✏️ Fix Words, type the correction, tap ✓ Keep Changes.
Your writing is yours. It works with no internet, and nobody else can see it. If Google Drive saving has been switched on for you, a copy of each book also appears in your own Google Drive, ready to print or share.
