# No Itinerary Summer — Instagram content plan

17 posts · 9 reels · 8 carousels · Aug 20 – Sep 27, 2026

Covers six weeks of travel: Morocco and Spain solo, a five-day laundry break,
a Florida → Los Angeles road trip with sister and daughter into the 4th of July,
then Banff from July 6.

---

## The one idea holding it together

**Anchor and empty middle.** Book the one thing that genuinely has to be booked —
the Toubkal guide, the Moraine Lake shuttle, the flight home — and leave everything
between them open. That's not a vibe, it's a repeatable method, and methods are what
people follow accounts for. Vibes get a like; a method gets a save and a follow.

Every caption below contains at least one line of *proof the trip was unplanned*:
booked nine hours out, changed cities on a whim, found it by accident, no hotel until
4pm. That recurring beat converts a stranger who likes a photo into a follower.

Stamp the series name on every cover — `NO ITINERARY SUMMER / 01` through `/ 17` —
so the grid reads as one body of work.

---

## Seven mechanics that do the actual work

1. **A reel opens a place, a carousel closes it.** Reels reach strangers; carousels
   convert them. Lead each location with a reel, land the carousel 2–3 days later
   while the reel is still circulating.
2. **Let the audience pick the order.** Post 01 is a trailer ending on "which stop
   first?" Follow the comments for posts 02–04.
3. **Collab every family post.** Instagram Collab publishes to two grids and pools
   engagement. Every Act II and III post should be a collab with your sister.
4. **Split save-bait and share-bait.** Logistics carousels (05, 13, 16) are saves.
   Laundry reel, vortex bit, Gaudí joke are shares. One of each per week minimum.
5. **Ask a question only one person can answer.** Not "thoughts?" — "tell me the city
   you haven't booked and I'll tell you what I'd do with the first 48 hours."
6. **Natural sound is a retention weapon.** Essaouira wind, Benirràs drums, glacial
   meltwater at full volume with trending audio ducked underneath. Comedy reels invert
   it: trending sound up front, joke carried by on-screen text.
7. **Write for search.** Plain place name in the first line, alt text filled, real
   location tag, 3–5 hashtags (one big, one mid, one niche, one community).

---

## Run of show

Three posts a week. Tuesday and Thursday for reels, Sunday for carousels (Sunday is
when people scroll photo dumps slowly). Three strong posts beat six thin ones — if a
week gets away from you, cut a post, never rush one.

**Caption**

> 4,167 metres. 13,671 feet. The highest point in North Africa and the Arab world, and
> for roughly ninety seconds up there, the highest point in my personality.
>
> We left the refuge at 4:30am in a line of headlamps. My guide ate one sandwich and
> walked up like he was going to check the mail. I brought seven snacks and the emotional
> range of a Victorian widow.
>
> Things that surprised me:
> — The trailhead at Imlil sits around 1,740m, so you climb roughly two and a half
> kilometres of vertical over two days
> — A licensed guide isn't a nice-to-have in Toubkal National Park, it's the law
> — The mules carry the gear and they are better at this than all of us
> — At the top there's a metal pyramid frame, and on a clear morning you can see the haze
> where the Sahara starts
>
> Full breakdown — cost, kit, refuge, timings — in the next post.
>
> Did I get emotional at the summit marker? No. That was the wind. 🌬️

**Why it works:** highest-ceiling post of the summer — a hard number, real physical stakes,
a visual payoff. Put your best editing hours here and give the summit shot room to breathe
instead of cutting on the beat.

travel content.

> Which city has the best free viewpoint? Barcelona set a high bar. 📍Barcelona

anywhere.

# Act III — Canadian Rockies
*3 posts · Sep 22 – 27 · Banff, from July 6*

## 15 · Reel · Tue Sep 22 · The turquoise reveal

**Hook:** "Two days ago we were watching fireworks in Los Angeles."

**Shot list**
- Hold the LA fireworks frame one beat, then hard cut to the lake — the whiplash is the hook
- Slow reveal on Moraine or Lake Louise, no music for the first two seconds, just water
- Hands in the water, for scale and the cold reaction
- Peaks, then all three of you small in the frame

**Caption**

> Two days after watching fireworks over Los Angeles we were standing in front of water
> this colour, and I'm still not sure I believe it happened.
>
> The turquoise isn't a filter and it isn't minerals. It's rock flour — glaciers grind
> bedrock into a powder so fine it never settles, stays suspended in the meltwater, and
> scatters blue-green light straight back at you. These lakes are full of pulverised
> mountain. That's it. That's the whole trick.
>
> Also: Banff was Canada's first national park, protected in 1885 because three railway
> workers stumbled onto hot springs and immediately started arguing over who owned them.
> The government's solution was to take the whole thing and make it a park. Genuinely
> iconic conflict resolution.
>
> Heads up if you're going — you can't drive your own car to Moraine Lake any more.
> Shuttle, bus or bike only, and it books out. Full logistics in the next post, because
> this is the one place where "no plan" will actually cost you the trip.
>
> 📍Banff National Park, Alberta

**Why it works:** the LA-to-glacier cut is the strongest transition available to you all
summer — use it. The rock flour explanation is the most shareable fact in this plan; lead
with it.

## 16 · Carousel · Thu Sep 24 · Banff logistics — **save-bait**

**Slides**

**Caption**

> I don't plan trips. Banff is the exception, and here's exactly where to spend your
> planning energy so you can improvise everything else.
>
> The non-negotiables:

>

> with no plan at all and it might be the most beautiful in North America — 230-odd

## Housekeeping

**Decide your daughter's privacy rule once, before post 10.** Face-on, back-of-head only,
or no appearances. Pick one and hold it rather than deciding post by post. The plan works
with any of the three — if it's no faces, her voice and her one-line reviews carry the
character just as well.

**Stories, three per posting day.** One raw behind-the-scenes clip that didn't make the
cut, one poll or question sticker tied to that day's post, one share of the post with an
arrow. Archive each act into a Highlight — Morocco, Spain, Road Trip, Rockies.

**In October, mine the top three.** Whichever three posts outperform, re-cut them: a reel
becomes a carousel, a carousel becomes a reel, a good caption line becomes its own post.
Reposting winners to a now-larger audience beats making new mediocre content.

**Fill in the blanks before you post.** Exact mileage, exact costs, your guide's name, the
town you actually stayed in on the coast, your daughter's real reviews. Specific numbers
are the difference between a caption that reads as lived and one that reads as written.
